Partner Reflections

How collaboration, candidate responsibilities, and campaign management shaped our understanding of political parties.

Derek Fitzgerald — Presidential Candidate

For this assignment, I worked with my partner, Henry, to develop the American Liberty Party, a fictional political party based on constitutional conservatism. I served as the presidential candidate, while Henry served as the campaign manager. Together, our goal was to create a political party with a consistent identity, a clear platform, and a campaign strategy.

One of the most challenging aspects of creating a unified platform was making sure our different policy positions fit together under the same political philosophy. We had to consider issues such as constitutional rights, economic policy, national defense, and immigration while maintaining a consistent message. I realized that even when people share similar political beliefs, they may have different priorities or approaches to specific issues.

Our assigned roles also influenced how we approached the project. As the presidential candidate, I focused primarily on the party's values, policy positions, and overall direction. Henry's role as campaign manager involved considering how the party would organize its campaign and communicate its platform to the public.

This assignment helped me better understand how political parties function as organizations rather than simply groups of people who share political beliefs. Developing a unified platform requires communication, cooperation, and compromise. I also learned that political campaigns involve much more than choosing a candidate, including fundraising, volunteer coordination, and developing a consistent public message.

Henry Jacobsen — Campaign Manager

My partner and candidate for president is Derek Fitzgerald. The most difficult part about creating a unified platform was deciding what topics to emphasize. There are many options when it comes to possible subjects that we see are important for the people to understand about our ideas for the nation. I think that the 10 matters that we covered represent both our views fairly well. It taught me about how parties work because we needed to decide what was most important and what kind of voters we want to catch with our platmform and what kind of campaign we run. I found that something I wanted from our campaign that I think is vitally important was making it as clear to the voters what we believed and that we would want to be held accountable by the people after Derek got into office.
