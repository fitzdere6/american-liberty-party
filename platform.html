What We Stand For

Our Ten-Point Platform

Ten concise policy planks spanning constitutional rights, economic and social priorities, domestic policy, and foreign affairs.

01 · Constitutional Rights

Constitutional Liberties

The American Liberty Party supports protecting the freedoms guaranteed by the Constitution, including speech, religion, assembly, and due process. We believe government power must remain limited by constitutional checks and balances, with independent courts protecting individual rights.

02 · Constitutional Rights

Second Amendment Rights

We support the constitutional right of law-abiding Americans to keep and bear arms. Our platform emphasizes lawful firearm ownership, responsible handling, and enforcement of existing laws while opposing restrictions that we believe unnecessarily burden constitutional rights.

03 · Economic Policy

Fiscal Responsibility

We support responsible federal budgeting, greater transparency in government spending, and long-term efforts to reduce deficits. Our party favors reviewing federal programs for effectiveness while maintaining essential services and honoring existing commitments.

04 · Economic Policy

Small Businesses and Economic Opportunity

We believe entrepreneurship, competition, and private investment are important drivers of economic opportunity. We support simplifying regulations, reducing unnecessary administrative burdens, and maintaining a predictable tax environment for small businesses.

05 · Social Policy

Education and Parental Involvement

We support greater parental involvement in education and increased flexibility for states and local school districts. Our platform emphasizes academic achievement, vocational education, school accountability, and educational opportunities suited to students’ different needs.

06 · Social Policy

Family and Community Support

We support policies that help families maintain financial stability, including access to affordable housing, childcare options, and community resources. We favor cooperation among families, nonprofit organizations, businesses, and state governments rather than relying exclusively on federal programs.

07 · Domestic Policy

Border Security and Immigration Reform

We support securing national borders, enforcing immigration laws, and improving the efficiency of lawful immigration pathways. Our platform also calls for consistent due process, practical visa reforms, and clear procedures for employers and applicants.

08 · Domestic Policy

Digital Privacy and Cybersecurity

We support stronger protections for personal information, transparent government surveillance practices, and judicial oversight of searches. Our party also prioritizes cybersecurity for critical infrastructure, federal systems, and private organizations while encouraging innovation and responsible data stewardship.

09 · Foreign Policy

National Defense and Foreign Relations

We support a capable military, readiness investments, and cooperation with international allies. Our foreign policy would prioritize national security, diplomacy, clear objectives for overseas commitments, and congressional oversight of military action.

10 · Domestic and Energy Policy

Energy Independence and Environmental Stewardship

We support reliable and affordable domestic energy production, including nuclear power, natural gas, and renewable technologies. Our platform favors technological innovation, responsible resource management, and environmental protections that account for economic and community impacts.
